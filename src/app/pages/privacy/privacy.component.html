3. Cookies and tracking

This website does not set any cookies of its own and does not use analytics, advertising or tracking tools. For this reason, no cookie banner is displayed.

4. Who else receives data

To run this website, I rely on the following service providers. They only process data needed for the service they provide.

  • Hosting (Hostinger) : serves the website and keeps technical server logs.
  • Email service : when you submit the contact form, the hosting provider's PHP mail service forwards the content of your message to my mailbox.
  • My email provider (Google Gmail) : receives the forwarded message and stores it in my mailbox.
  • GitHub : the "Projects" section loads my public repositories directly from GitHub's servers, which means your browser contacts GitHub (and its image servers) when the section is displayed.
  • Google Fonts and icon providers : fonts and icons (Google Fonts, Boxicons) are loaded from external servers when a page is displayed.

Whenever your browser loads a resource from one of these third parties, your IP address is necessarily transmitted to them. Some of these providers are located outside the European Economic Area (notably in the United States). In that case, the transfer relies on the safeguards provided by the GDPR, such as an adequacy decision or standard contractual clauses.

5. How long data is kept

  • Contact messages : kept as long as needed to answer you and follow up on the conversation, then deleted at most 12 months after our last exchange.
  • Server logs : kept by the hosting provider for a limited period, according to its own retention policy.
